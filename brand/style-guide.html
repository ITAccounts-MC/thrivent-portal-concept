Brand asset & style guide

A portal that knows your season, and the good you do in it

The visual system for the Thrivent life-stage portal concept, derived from thrivent.com's live styles and the supplied logos.

Inspected Nov 12, 2026 · thrivent.com home + /generosity/membership · 4 page captures · Concept by Modern Climate. Illustrative only.

01 · Character

Warm, calm, and people first

Thrivent's site balances financial credibility with generosity. Generosity stats get the same visual weight as financial ones, and red is used rarely, so it always means "act here."

Warm over corporate

Cream and sand washes alternate with cool mist panels. White is home base.

Real people, real impact

Members serving in their communities, in arch and circle crops, with named member quotes.

Where Money Means More™

The tagline sits in the gold footer band beside the monogram, linking money to meaning.

02 · Logo

Wordmark and monogram

Clear space is at least the height of the red cap on all sides. The wordmark is never under 96px wide and the monogram never under 24px tall. Never recolor, stretch or add effects to the mark.

06 · Motifs, layout & imagery

The monogram pattern and the arch

Monogram pattern

The t-cap shape in thin gold outline on cream, with one filled red accent. Used behind heroes.

Arch & circle crops

Hero photos as large arches; stat callouts in white circles overlapping the photo.

Layout

Container 1248px (wide 1408px) · 64–96px between sections · backgrounds alternate white → mist → white → sand → navy · radius 4 / 6 / 8 / 16 / pill · card shadow 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0,0,0,.08)

Imagery

Candid, multigenerational, natural light, people serving. For the concept, use illustrated avatars or licensed photos; don't reuse photos of real members from the site.

08 · Do & don't

Do

  • Lead with people and community impact
  • Use the light-weight display headline
  • Pair warm (sand) and cool (mist) neutrals
  • Keep red to one action per screen
  • Sentence case; arrows on text links

Don't

  • Use the legacy heart-cross mark
  • Use #E30045 in UI (logo only)
  • Use heavy shadows, gradients or all-caps headlines
  • Reuse photos of real members from thrivent.com
  • Present the concept as a live Thrivent product
