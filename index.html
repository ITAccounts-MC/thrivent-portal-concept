The benefits are strong: grief care, caregiver support, identity protection, Choice Dollars. But they sit on a list, not in a member's life. And the neighbors who serve on Action Teams rarely hear from Thrivent again.

The relationship model

A connected membership flywheel.

Each priority strengthens the next, and generosity sits at the heart of all three.

  1. 1
    Equipped advisors inspire participation.Advisors see what a member cares about, with permission, and start there.In Kari’s story: Travis opens with her mother, not a product.
  2. 2
    Meaningful participation builds community.Members serve, give and lead in ways that fit their season.In Kari’s story: she leads a coat drive and directs her Choice Dollars.
  3. 3
    A growing community creates new relationships for advisors to support.Neighbors on Action Teams keep their impact, and many choose to join.In Kari’s story: 30 people she served with became members.
A personalized member portal and ongoing communicationsconnect the experience and make each next step more relevant. One rule holds it together: advisors never see guest data.

    Step 1

    Kari joins a neighbor’s Action Team.

    Sarah, who lives down the street, is leading a food-packing team. Kari isn’t a Thrivent member. When she signs up, she chooses to hear what the team accomplished. That gets her a simple guest page, with no sales pitch.

    Step 2

    Her impact is waiting for her.

    Her guest page now shows her hours and a Choice Dollars credit, kept for her if she joins. Joining is her choice, and no advisor sees this page.

    Try it: press Become a member →
    Step 1

    She tells Thrivent what she cares about.

    Three causes are enough to reshape her home: the teams she sees, the organizations suggested for her Choice Dollars, the stories she gets.

    Try it: pick three causes →
    Step 2

    A home built around her causes.

    Benefits show up as they become useful. At this stage that means identity protection and a first Thrivent Bank account.

    Step 1

    Kari and Dan link their household.

    Their impact becomes shared, and so does the season: a new baby, a coverage review, and family-sized ways to serve.

    Step 2

    Their household's Choice Dollars are split across the causes they share, with a live preview of what each share funds.

    Try it: move the sliders →
    Step 1

    Kari leads her first Action Team.

    Leading a team unlocks a Leader hub in her portal. It's built around the people coming, not the paperwork.

    Try it: press Send invites in the portal →
    Step 2

    Neighbors say yes, and seven of them aren't members.

    Guests choose whether to hear about the team's impact. Their contact details stay with Thrivent and never reach an advisor.

    Try it: direct the $85 in Choice Dollars →

    How it works

    One orchestration layer decides who hears what, when, and from whom.

    Benefits never sell products.Benefits and finances sit in separate panels; no message pairs them.
    Members declare; nothing sensitive is inferred.Seasons like caregiving or loss come only from the member.
    Guests stay guests until they choose.Opt-in at sign-up, no advisor access, and "never joins" is a good outcome too.
    No nurture under 16.Youth teams are welcome; their data stays out.
